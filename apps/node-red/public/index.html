Prototype boundaries

Evacuva uses simulated sensors and experimental safety thresholds. The map demonstrates scalable route processing for university evaluation; it is not a certified emergency-navigation system. The recorded trials show strong improvement, but they do not prove a guaranteed five-second service level.
